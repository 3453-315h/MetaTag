## 2024-05-24 - PySide6 Accessibility Learnings\n**Learning:** In PySide6, QFormLayout.addRow() automatically establishes buddy relationships between labels and inputs. For other layouts like QHBoxLayout or QVBoxLayout, explicitly call label.setBuddy(widget) to enable screen reader associations, and use an ampersand (&) in the label text to assign keyboard accelerators (e.g., '&Search:' for Alt+S).\n**Action:** Apply this to standalone labels in custom layouts.
